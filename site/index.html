How well do language models understand real Telugu?

A small benchmark of questions written directly in Telugu, not translated from English, and grounded in Telangana and Andhra culture. It tests three things: cultural knowledge, Telugu–English code-mixed text as people actually type it (in Telugu script and Romanized), and reading comprehension.
